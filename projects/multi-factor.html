A-share multi-factor stock selection

Personal research project, 2026 · Code on GitHub

An end-to-end research stack for Chinese equities: point-in-time data, factor evaluation, linear and machine-learning ranking models, and execution rules that match what a broker will actually fill. Each part has a baseline and a check designed to catch my own mistakes.

1. Data that only knows the past

  • Free BaoStock and AkShare data for the CSI 300 and CSI 500 universe, using historical index constituents rather than today's list.
  • Fundamentals keyed to announcement dates, falling back to the statutory deadline; reports older than 270 days are treated as stale.
  • 14 factors across value, size, momentum, reversal, low volatility, low turnover, quality and growth.

3. LightGBM ranking

A 12-factor strategy originally written for JoinQuant, ported to run locally on the free data. LightGBM is trained on 18 non-overlapping cross-sections, and every label window ends before the decision date.

  • The hand-set linear weights treat momentum and money-flow factors as positive, but 10 of the 12 factors had negative IC here. LightGBM learns the reversal from past data instead.
  • Leakage check: trained on shuffled labels, the same pipeline gives a mean out-of-sample IC of -0.001.
  • Caveat I found later: this run's rebalance schedule was one of the luckier start days. Across all 20 possible start days the median is much lower. See the timing study.

4. Execution that matches the broker

On JoinQuant the first version sat on 34% cash. STAR Market stocks need at least 200 shares per order, and rounding every order down to 100 shares left high-priced stocks at zero. I rewrote the order logic:

  • Each stock's lot rule is applied when sizing.
  • Overweight positions are trimmed before buying.
  • Leftover cash goes into the most underweight names, one lot at a time.
  • Fills that fail on halted or limit-up days are retried daily.

In simulation, idle cash fell to about 0.5%.
