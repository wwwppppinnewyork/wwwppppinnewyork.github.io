How much of a backtest is the calendar?

LightGBMA-sharesBacktest design

The same LightGBM top-20 strategy, started on each of the 20 possible days of a 20-day rebalance cycle, plus tests of signal decay, holding period and signal-triggered trading.

The start day alone moved annual return across a range of more than 10 percentage points. My earlier headline result was one of the lucky offsets.

A-share multi-factor stock selection

Factor researchXGBoost / LightGBMJoinQuant

A full pipeline from point-in-time data to tradable orders: 14 factors on free BaoStock / AkShare data, linear baselines (z-score, ICIR weighting, WLS), gradient-boosted ranking models, and JoinQuant strategies with lot-aware order sizing.

Linear composites with hand-set signs had negative IC in CSI 300 + 500; LightGBM recovered the reversal effects. A placebo on shuffled labels gives IC of -0.001.

Reinforcement learningPyTorchRobustness

Research internship project (2024): technical-indicator features and a DQN agent trading one stock, reviewed in 2026, with nine bugs fixed and the backtest rerun over 10 random seeds.

Is the Nasdaq-100 premium alpha or factor risk?

RegressionFama-FrenchColumbia University

Columbia University. QQQ vs SPY, 1999-2026, with CAPM, three-factor and five-factor regressions, diagnostics, Newey-West inference and out-of-sample tests.

CAPM explains QQQ's premium (alpha p = 0.73). Under FF5 its anti-value, anti-profitability tilts leave a significant +4.7% a year.
